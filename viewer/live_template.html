Inside its head

Scoresheet · bar = time spent

Homo Ludens playing itself on a simulated clock. At 1× you see each move after exactly the time it chose to spend; the faster speeds compress that. Its moves, think times and thoughts were recorded live on the engine; this page replays them at the pace it played. Every line on the right is the engine's own trace: what it noticed, which moves it considered, how deep it calculated, its blunder check, and the time it chose to spend.
